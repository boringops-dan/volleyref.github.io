QUESTIONS REFS ACTUALLY ASK

Before you blow the first whistle

Is it really free to try?
Your first 3 matches are free with every feature. No credit card.
Which rulesets are supported?
FIVB, USAV, NFHS, and NCAA. Pick yours at match setup and the app enforces it.
Do I need to install anything?
No. It runs in any browser. App Store and Play Store versions exist if you prefer them.
Can I export official scoresheets?
Yes, an official PDF the moment the match ends. Print it or send it to the league.
How does it handle subs and the libero?
Automatically. Limits, positions, wait-a-rally, exceptional subs after injury or DQ. Illegal actions are blocked.
How long has it been around?
Since 2010. Sixteen years of continuous development and real matches.
