Libero "Teleportation"

Normally the libero waits one rally before returning. In libero-serving leagues there is an exception when the libero rotates back in to serve. VolleyRef tracks the libero serving position and keeps it consistent.

Sanctions & Disqualifications

Warnings. Penalties. Expulsions. DQs. When a player is disqualified, the exceptional substitution rules kick in automatically. No rulebook lookup required.

QUESTIONS REFS ACTUALLY ASK

Before you blow the first whistle

Is it really free to try?
Your first 3 matches are free with every feature. No credit card.
Which rulesets are supported?
FIVB, USAV, NFHS, and NCAA. Pick yours at match setup and the app enforces it.
Do I need to install anything?
No. It runs in any browser. An iPhone and iPad version is available from the App Store; Android works in the browser.

Automatically. Limits, positions, wait-a-rally, exceptional subs after injury or DQ. Illegal actions are blocked.
