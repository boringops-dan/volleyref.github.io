Rule Enforcement

Every Snow Rule. Enforced.

Pick the Snow ruleset at setup. Point targets, side changes, timeouts, and roster limits all switch to match FIVB Snow Volleyball rules.

Best of 3, and unlike indoor volleyball every set including the decider is played to 15, won by a minimum 2-point lead with no cap. The app never applies the indoor 25-point target by mistake.

Teams switch on the combined total every 5 points, in every set including the decider, to offset shifting wind, sun, and snow. The app tracks the total and prompts the change.

Snow is played 3 versus 3 from a roster capped at 4. Each team gets up to 2 substitutions per set, and a returning player may come back in a different position. The app enforces the count and the looser re-entry rule.

Snow volleyball has no libero and no six-zone rotation. Players hold static positions, so the libero and rotation surfaces disappear from the match screen entirely.

Each team gets 1 timeout per set rather than the 2 allowed indoors. Intervals between sets run 1 minute, with a 3-minute warmup.

Snow Is Not Indoor, and Not Beach.

Three players instead of six or two. Every set to 15 rather than 25 with a 15-point decider. Ends changed every 5 points. Two subs per set where beach allows none. Running snow on an indoor preset means overriding almost every option, and forgetting one costs you a set.

Built for Cold Hands and No Signal.

Mountain venues rarely have reliable data. The app works fully offline and syncs when signal returns, so a snow tournament runs exactly like an indoor one.

Rules Q&A

Snow Volleyball Rules, Answered.

How many players are on the court in snow volleyball?

3 versus 3, from a roster capped at 4 players.

How is snow volleyball scored?

Best of 3, with every set including the decider played to 15 by a minimum 2-point lead and no cap.

How many substitutions and timeouts are allowed in snow volleyball?

Up to 2 substitutions and 1 timeout per set, fewer than indoor FIVB allows.

Does snow volleyball have a libero?

No. Every player on the roster plays both offense and defense.

Snow volleyball, defined

The full glossary entry: court, format, roster, timeouts, and side changes in one place.

How does beach volleyball scoring differ?

2 versus 2 on sand, sets to 21, ends changed every 7 points, and no substitutions at all.
