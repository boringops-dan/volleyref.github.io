Rule Enforcement

Every Beach Rule. Enforced.

Pick the Beach ruleset at setup. Sets, side switches, timeouts, and roster limits all change to match. No settings to remember.

Best of 3. The first two sets run to 21, the third to 15, both won by a minimum 2-point lead with no cap. The app knows which set you are in and never ends one early.

Teams switch on the combined total: every 7 points in sets 1 and 2, every 5 in the decider. The app tracks the running total and prompts the switch. You keep your eyes on the court.

Beach rosters are exactly two players and there are no substitutions. The app hides the substitution controls entirely on the Beach ruleset rather than letting you record a sub that does not exist in the rulebook.

There is no libero on the sand, and no six-zone rotation to police. Players hold static positions and alternate service order. The libero and rotation surfaces disappear so the screen shows only what a beach referee needs.

Each team gets 1 timeout per set, 30 seconds long, against 2 per set indoors. Try to record a second and the app stops you. Intervals between sets run 1 minute.

Beach Is Not Indoor.

Different point targets. Different side switches. No subs, no libero, no rotation. Running a beach match on an indoor scoring app means overriding half the settings and remembering the rest yourself. Select Beach in VolleyRef.App and every one of those differences is already handled.

Built for Sand, Sun, and One Phone.

Tournament desk on a beach, no power, patchy signal. The app works fully offline and syncs when it can. Score from a phone in one hand while the crowd follows the live scoreboard on theirs.

Rules Q&A

Beach Volleyball Rules, Answered.

How many players are on the court in beach volleyball?

2 versus 2, with no substitutions and no libero. Each roster is exactly two players.

How is beach volleyball scored?

Best of 3. Sets 1 and 2 to 21, the deciding set to 15, each won by a minimum 2-point lead with no cap.

When do teams switch sides in beach volleyball?

Every 7 points in sets 1 and 2, and every 5 points in the deciding third set.

How many substitutions and timeouts are allowed in beach volleyball?

No substitutions at all, and 1 timeout of 30 seconds per team per set.

Does beach volleyball have a libero?

No. With two players per side, both serve, both attack, and both defend.
