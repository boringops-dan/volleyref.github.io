# Transition - Volleyball Glossary

> Transition is the shift from one phase of play to another, most often from defense back to offense. It also covers the move from serve-receive into the offensive system. "Transition" is a tactical term, not defined in FIVB rules. Good transition play requires players to quickly move from their defensive positions to their offensive approach paths after a dig. Back-row attack rules (FIVB Rule 13.3.2) apply equally in transition as in serve-receive.

**Ruleset:** FIVB
**Rule Reference:** FIVB Rule 13 (attack hit)
