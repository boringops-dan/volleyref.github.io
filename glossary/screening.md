# Screening - Volleyball Glossary

> Screening is an illegal action in which the serving team obstructs the opponents' view of the server or the ball. It is judged at the moment of serve. Per FIVB Rule 12.5.1, it is a fault for players to jump, wave their arms, or move together in a line to screen the receiver. Individual players standing legally in their rotational position are not considered screening. A screening fault results in a point and serve for the opponent.

**Ruleset:** FIVB
**Rule Reference:** FIVB Rule 12.5.1
