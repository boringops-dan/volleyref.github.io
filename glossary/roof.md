# Roof (Stuff Block) - Volleyball Glossary

> A roof is a dominant stuff block driven straight down into the attacker's court for an immediate point. The blocker's hands are positioned above the ball. "Roof" and "stuff block" are colloquial terms, not defined in FIVB rules. A stuff block is legal and scores a point for the blocking team if the ball lands in bounds on the attacking team's court (FIVB Rule 14.1.1). After a block, the blocking team's next contact counts as their first of three.

**Ruleset:** FIVB
**Rule Reference:** FIVB Rule 14.1.1
