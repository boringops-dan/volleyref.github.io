# Attack Line (3-Meter Line) - Volleyball Glossary

> The attack line is a line drawn 3 meters from and parallel to the center line on each side of the court. Per FIVB Rules 1.3.4 and 1.4.1, the attack line, whose rear edge is 3 m from the axis of the centre line, marks the front zone (net side) off from the back zone. Front-row players (positions 2, 3, 4) may attack and block from anywhere in the front zone. Back-row players may not complete an attack hit where the ball is entirely above the net if their takeoff occurred at or in front of the attack line (FIVB Rules 13.2.2 and 13.3.3). The attack line extends to the sidelines and is considered part of the front zone.

**Ruleset:** FIVB
**Rule Reference:** FIVB Rule 1.3.4; FIVB Rule 1.4.1
