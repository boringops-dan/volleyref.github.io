# Float Serve - Volleyball Glossary

> A float serve is struck without spin, so the ball travels on an unpredictable, knuckleball-like path. Air resistance acts asymmetrically on the seams, making the flight wobble. Float serves are not separately defined in FIVB rules but are governed by general service rules (FIVB Rule 12). The serve is legal provided the server contacts the ball cleanly within the service zone. The server contacts the centre of the ball with a firm, flat hand and stops the arm swing short, which keeps spin off the ball. It can be hit standing or as a jump float. Float serves are popular at all levels because the unpredictable movement is difficult to pass accurately, and they are widely used in high school and club play.

**Ruleset:** FIVB
**Rule Reference:** FIVB Rule 12
