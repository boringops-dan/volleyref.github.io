# Sideout - Volleyball Glossary

> A sideout (side out) occurs when the receiving team wins the rally and gains the right to serve. Under rally scoring (the current system), a point is awarded on every rally, so a sideout earns the receiving team a point and the serve. The team then rotates one position clockwise before serving, and the player moving from position 2 to position 1 serves next (FIVB Rule 7.6.2). The term "sideout" comes from the old sideout scoring system, in which only the serving team could score and winning a rally as the receiving team earned only the serve. Coaches track sideout percentage, the share of opponent serves a team turns into a sideout, as a measure of its serve-receive offence.

**Ruleset:** FIVB
**Rule Reference:** FIVB Rule 6.1.3.2; FIVB Rule 7.6.2
