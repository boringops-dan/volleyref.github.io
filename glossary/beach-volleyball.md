# Beach Volleyball - Volleyball Glossary

> Beach volleyball is a variant of volleyball played 2 versus 2 on a sand court, contested at the Olympic Games and on the FIVB Beach Pro Tour. Per FIVB Beach Volleyball rules each team fields exactly 2 players, with no substitutions and no libero, so the pair that starts a match finishes it. Matches are best-of-3: the first two sets are played to 21 points and the deciding set to 15, each won by a minimum 2-point lead with no point cap. Each team gets 1 timeout of 30 seconds per set, and the interval between sets is 1 minute. Teams change ends every 7 points in the first two sets and every 5 points in the deciding set to even out sun and wind. Players have no set court positions and may stand anywhere; they only keep the service order the captain fixes after the toss, instead of rotating through the six indoor court zones.
