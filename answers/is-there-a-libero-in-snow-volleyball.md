# Does snow volleyball have a libero?

**Short Answer:** No. Snow volleyball has no libero position; all players on the 3-player roster play both offense and defense.

## Full Explanation

Unlike indoor volleyball, which designates a defensive specialist libero under FIVB Rule 19, snow volleyball has no libero position. With only 3 players per side and a 4-player roster cap, every player is available to attack, block, and serve without the positional restrictions that apply to an indoor libero.

**Rulesets:** FIVB
**Rule Reference:** FIVB Snow Volleyball Rules
