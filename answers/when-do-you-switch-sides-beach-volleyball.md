# When do teams switch sides in beach volleyball?

**Short Answer:** Teams change ends every 7 points in sets 1 and 2, and every 5 points in the deciding third set.

## Full Explanation

Per FIVB Beach Volleyball rules, teams change ends on the combined total of both scores: every 7 points in the first two sets, so at 7, 14, 21 and onward, and every 5 points in the deciding set, so at 5, 10, 15 and onward. Changing this often keeps sun, wind, and court conditions from favoring one end for long. Indoor volleyball has no equivalent: teams change ends between sets, plus once in the deciding set when the leading team reaches 8.
