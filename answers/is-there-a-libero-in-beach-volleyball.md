# Does beach volleyball have a libero?

**Short Answer:** No. Beach volleyball has no libero. With two players per side, both serve, both attack, and both defend.

## Full Explanation

Beach volleyball has no libero position. The libero is an indoor specialization built around the six-player rotation and back-row attack restrictions under FIVB Rule 19, and neither of those exists in the 2-player beach game. Both players serve in alternating order, both may attack from anywhere, and both play defense. Snow volleyball has no libero either. Select the Beach ruleset in VolleyRef.App and the libero controls disappear from the match surface entirely, so there is nothing to mis-tap.
