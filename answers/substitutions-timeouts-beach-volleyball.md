# How many substitutions and timeouts are allowed in beach volleyball?

**Short Answer:** Beach volleyball allows no substitutions at all, and 1 timeout of 30 seconds per team per set.

## Full Explanation

Per FIVB Beach Volleyball rules there are no substitutions. Each roster is exactly two players and those two finish the match they started; if a player cannot continue, the team is declared incomplete and the set or match is awarded to the opponent. Each team gets 1 timeout per set, lasting 30 seconds, against 2 timeouts per set indoors. The interval between sets is 1 minute.
